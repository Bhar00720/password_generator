Frequently Asked Questions

How does this password generator ensure cryptographic security?

We use the browser native window.crypto.getRandomValues() API (a Cryptographically Secure Pseudo-Random Number Generator or CSPRNG) rather than Math.random(), ensuring entropy suitable for protecting sensitive accounts.

What is the difference between a random password and a passphrase?

A random password consists of randomized alphanumeric and special characters (e.g., "xK9#m$L2!p"). A passphrase consists of multiple randomly chosen words separated by hyphens or spaces (e.g., "correct-horse-battery-staple"), providing high entropy while remaining human-memorable.

Are generated passwords ever stored or sent across the internet?

Never. The generator runs 100% locally in your browser memory. No generated passwords, seeds, or settings are ever transmitted to any server or recorded in any log file.

What password length is recommended for optimal security?

Security standards recommend at least 16 characters for random passwords combining uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols, or 4+ words for passphrases to achieve over 80+ bits of entropy.
